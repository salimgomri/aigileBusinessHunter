## QUALIFICATION

Evaluate:
- seniority
- responsibilities
- Agile/Scrum/SAFe relevance
- transformation scope
- coaching/facilitation
- project/programme management
- delivery
- banking/financial-services relevance
- stakeholder/executive exposure
- location
- language requirements
- mandatory requirements
- major gaps

Do not use a score unless the score is reproducible from explicit criteria.
Prefer a concise compatibility assessment.

## FRESHNESS

Classify:
- CONFIRMED RECENT
- RECENTLY INDEXED
- DATE UNKNOWN
- OLD

Rules:
- OLD cannot be HOT.
- DATE UNKNOWN cannot be HOT.
- An offer with uncertain active status goes to À VÉRIFIER.
- Never claim an offer is still open unless the available evidence supports that claim.

## CONTACTS

Look for:
- recruiter
- Talent Acquisition
- hiring manager
- Head of Transformation
- relevant business owner

Never guess an email.
A name found in an article is not automatically a recruiter or hiring manager.

## REPORT LIMITS

Maximum 5 main opportunities.
Separate:
- HOT
- WARM
- À VÉRIFIER

À VÉRIFIER does not count as a qualified opportunity.

## GMAIL

Maximum 3 drafts per run.
One opportunity = one draft.
Never auto-send.

Report:
- recipient
- exact subject
- draft status

Never simulate draft creation.
