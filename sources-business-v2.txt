## SIGNAL PRIORITY

VERY STRONG:
- explicit Agile Coach / Agile Transformation / SAFe need
- freelance mission matching Salim
- Agile at Scale / CoE / transformation programme
- explicit change/transformation mandate
- tender clearly involving transformation, delivery, project/programme/change management
- new CIO/CTO/CPO/COO connected to a concrete transformation context

MEDIUM:
- major digital transformation
- reorganisation
- migration / modernization
- new transformation leadership
- strong technology change with an identifiable owner

WEAK — DO NOT QUALIFY ALONE:
- generic AI article
- fundraising alone
- generic company growth
- isolated technical recruitment
- generic digitalisation news

## BUSINESS QUALIFICATION

Mandatory chain:
SIGNAL → PROOF → WHY NOW → PROBABLE PROBLEM → DECISION MAKER → OFFER → APPROACH

No PROOF = no HOT.

A company is not a prospect merely because it appears in an article.

## CONTACT

Prioritize the person who owns the problem:
- CIO
- CTO
- CPO
- COO
- Head of Transformation
- Head of Agile
- Transformation Director
- HR/Talent Acquisition when the signal is an explicit hiring need

Do not automatically target the CEO.

Never guess an email.

## REPORT LIMITS

Maximum:
- 3 HOT
- 5 WARM
- 8 new qualified prospects total

Do not fill quotas artificially.

Add "À VÉRIFIER" for promising but insufficiently verified signals.
À VÉRIFIER does not count as HOT/WARM.

## GMAIL

Maximum 3 drafts per run.
One prospect = one draft.
Never auto-send.

A successful draft must be reported with:
- recipient
- exact subject
- status

If draft creation fails, say so.
Never simulate success.
